## Vocabulary Data

Spanish, French, English, and German word frequency lists are sourced from [FrequencyWords](https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords) by Hermit Dave (OpenSubtitles), licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

German lemma, gender, plural, and inflection data used to build `src/db/seeds/data/de_5k.json` comes from the [kaikki.org](https://kaikki.org/) machine-readable German dictionary (English-edition Wiktionary extract via [wiktextract](https://github.com/tatuylonen/wiktextract)), licensed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Cite: Tatu Ylonen, *Wiktextract: Wiktionary as Machine-Readable Structured Data*, LREC 2022.
